URLLC Remote Robot Control over Private 5G MEC

Undergraduate thesis · RAMO Lab, Yonsei University · Jan – Jun 2024

Real-time robot vision inference offloading and ultra-reliable low-latency (URLLC) remote control, implemented on a private 5G MEC testbed.

  • Offloaded vision inference (object detection and monocular depth estimation) from the robot to an edge/core server over a private 5G network
  • Implemented a real-time safety mechanism that halts and resumes the robot upon object intrusion

Adaptive UAV–MEC Collaborative Inference

Research project · Yonsei University · Mar – Jul 2026 · 🏆 Outstanding Presentation Award

A UAV–MEC split-computing pipeline for real-time object detection in urban traffic monitoring, robust under degraded wireless channels.

  • Designed adaptive split computing between the UAV and the edge server responding to wireless channel quality
  • Published at KIECS 2026 (Outstanding Paper Award); received the Outstanding Presentation Award at the 8th Industry–Academia Project Showcase, Yonsei University

UAV-Based Road Object Recognition for Digital Land-Information Mapping

Research Assistant · MOLIT-funded national R&D program · Jan 2025 – Present

A deep learning-based detection system for real-time recognition of road objects from UAV aerial imagery.

  • Improved detection of small, occluded, and low-light objects via tiling-based augmentation and EO/IR sensor fusion
