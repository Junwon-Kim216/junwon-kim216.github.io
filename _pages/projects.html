Adaptive UAV–MEC Collaborative Inference for Urban Traffic Monitoring

Connected Mobility Research Project (Graduate Course) · Yonsei University · Mar. – Jul. 2026

🏆 Outstanding Paper Award 🏆 Outstanding Oral Presentation Award

Built a UAV–MEC split-computing pipeline for real-time object detection in traffic monitoring that remains robust under poor wireless channels.

  • Evaluated the robustness of YOLO split computing under wireless signal-quality degradation
  • Published the results as a first-author paper at KIECS 2026, receiving the Outstanding Paper Award
  • Received the Outstanding Oral Presentation Award at the 8th Industry–Academia Project Showcase of the MOTIE/KIAT Future Vehicle Core Technology Workforce Consortium

UAV Aerial Imagery-Based Road Object Detection for Dynamic Thematic Map Generation

Research Assistant · Yonsei University · Jan. 2025 – Present

Part of the national R&D project “Development of Fixed/Moving Platform Based Dynamic Thematic Map Generation Technology for Next Generation Digital Land Information Construction,” funded by the Korea Agency for Infrastructure Technology Advancement (KAIA) under the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport (MOLIT).

  • Built a deep learning-based detection system for real-time recognition of road objects from UAV aerial imagery
  • Improved detection of small and occluded objects using tiling-based augmentation
  • Developed misalignment-tolerant EO/IR sensor fusion for robust low-light detection, meeting accuracy and latency targets in official certification testing

URLLC Remote Robot Control over Private 5G MEC

B.S. Thesis · RAMO Lab, Yonsei University · Jan. – Jun. 2024

Built a multi-access edge computing testbed for collaborative computing in ultra-reliable low-latency remote robot control over a private 5G system.

  • Offloaded robot vision inference to an edge/core server over a private 5G network
  • Deployed object detection and monocular depth estimation on the edge server
  • Implemented a real-time safety stop that halts the robot when an object enters its safety zone
